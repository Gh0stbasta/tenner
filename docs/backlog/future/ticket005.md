## Type

Product Strategy (Postponed)

---

## Priority

Low

## Phase

## Goal

Evaluate a marketplace where households share Tenner templates publicly.

---

# Background

Only meaningful with many households (FUTURE-003). Introduces moderation,
abuse, licensing and privacy questions (templates may reveal household details).
